# Business Rules and Thresholds

This is a code-based reference, not an approved fund mandate or regulatory interpretation. Thresholds are POC/demo values unless stated otherwise. “Why” describes the purpose implied by the implementation.

## Scope

The live API loads the five funds from `backend/app/data_v2.py`; `backend/app/data.py` is the synthetic legacy dataset and also supplies parts of the planner's universe. `backend/app/policy.py` evaluates generated orders. The planner additionally produces a separate concentration summary, so `policy_checks` is the broader pre-trade evaluation. Forecast returns are hard-coded placeholders, not live model predictions.

## Portfolio Exposure Controls

The policy calculates projected issuer, sector, group, and country exposure as a percentage of post-trade AUM. Exceeding a limit is normally `BLOCK`; reaching at least 90% of the limit is `WARN`; otherwise the result is `PASS`. For an inherited breach, a proposal that increases the breach by no more than 0.01 percentage points is `WARN`, rather than `BLOCK`.

| Active fund                       | Single issuer | Business group | Sector soft cap |
| --------------------------------- | ------------: | -------------: | --------------: |
| HDFC Flexi Cap                    |           10% |            20% |             35% |
| ICICI Prudential Large Cap        |           10% |            20% |             35% |
| SBI Nifty 50 ETF                  |           13% |            18% |             30% |
| HDFC Retirement Equity            |           10% |            18% |             30% |
| Kotak Large & Mid Cap             |           10% |            20% |             35% |
| Generic fallback / synthetic data |           10% |            20% |             35% |

**Rule purpose and usage:** issuer limits constrain one security's portfolio weight; group and sector limits control aggregate concentrations. They are evaluated in `policy._concentration_checks` using `data_v2.FUND_COMPLIANCE_LIMITS` for active funds. The displayed current-utilization status is separate: `BREACH` strictly above the cap, `WARN` at 90% or more, otherwise `OK` (`data_v2.compliance_utilization`).

**Important qualification:** active disclosure data assigns each security its ticker as its `group`, so cross-issuer business-group aggregation is not actually represented. The limits are POC settings, not verified real-world regulatory rules.

Other exposure settings configured by fund are minimum large-cap mix (65%, 80%, 100%, 75%, 70%, respectively) and maximum cash (15%, 12%, 5%, 12%, 14%). Generic fallback values are 80% and 20%. They are returned by the limits endpoint but are **not enforced** by policy or planner code.

## Order Eligibility and Execution Risk

| Rule / threshold        | How it is used                                                                                                                                                                                                  | Where and why                                                                                                                                                   |
| ----------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Restricted / pledged    | Any order is`BLOCK` if security is restricted or has pledged shares. Automated buy candidate selection also excludes it.                                                                                      | `policy._order_checks`, `buy_exclusion_reason`; prevent trading restricted or encumbered holdings. Defaults are false/zero.                                 |
| Watchlist               | `ESCALATE` for review.                                                                                                                                                                                        | `policy._order_checks`; require human review. No default watchlist names.                                                                                     |
| Stale price             | `BLOCK` an order.                                                                                                                                                                                             | `policy._order_checks`; avoid executable orders with stale pricing. Metadata defaults to non-stale and does not calculate age.                                |
| Sellable quantity       | Sell orders cannot exceed sellable shares less pending sells, or leave fewer than the configured minimum holding. Planner sell builders cap generated quantities to available shares.                           | `planner._available_sellable_shares`; `policy._order_checks`; prevent double-selling and preserve restricted quantities.                                    |
| Lock-in                 | Locked shares are unavailable for sale. Expiry is displayed but does not dynamically unlock shares. Lock percentages/expiry are curated per fund/security.                                                      | `data_v2.FUND_LOCK_RULES`; planner sell builders; preserve strategic positions. These are demo assumptions, not disclosure-derived restrictions.              |
| ESG exclusion           | New buys of excluded securities are`BLOCK`; holdings are not automatically sold. ITC and COALINDIA are excluded in POC metadata/config. Thermal power generation >20% adds a buy `WARN` (strictly greater). | `policy.py`, `security_metadata.py`; demonstrate exclusions and exposure review. Classification is illustrative, not reviewed ESG data.                     |
| Bid/ask spread          | <=0.5% passes; >0.5% through 1% warns; >1% blocks.                                                                                                                                                              | `policy._order_checks`; liquidity/market-impact proxy. Default spread is estimated from ADV bands.                                                            |
| Missing ADV             | `ESCALATE`.                                                                                                                                                                                                   | `policy._order_checks`; do not treat unverifiable execution capacity as a pass.                                                                               |
| ADV participation       | Effective ADV is min(ADV, median ADV), with median defaulting to 85% of ADV. Daily participation = order value / effective ADV / horizon. >5% warns; >75% escalates (not a hard block).                         | `policy._order_checks`; allow a parent order to be scheduled over the horizon. A comment mentions a 10% daily target, but the actual warning threshold is 5%. |
| Planner liquidity flag  | Separate non-blocking flag uses whole order / synthetic-universe ADV without horizon adjustment: >=10% gives MEDIUM risk; >=25% gives HIGH risk.                                                                | `planner._risk_flags`; can disagree with policy ADV result, especially for active v2 funds.                                                                   |
| Corporate-action timing | Dividend/ex-date within 1 day warns; other event within 2 days warns; boundary is inclusive. Separately, planner adds a MEDIUM timing flag if an event falls anywhere in the requested horizon.                 | `policy._order_checks`, `planner._risk_flags`; caution around event dates. Event data is curated, not live.                                                 |

## Plan-Level Escalation and Disposition

| Rule / threshold      | How it is used                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | Where and why                                                                                       |
| --------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Order count           | More than 50 orders warns; more than 100 escalates.                                                                                                                                                                                           | `policy._order_checks`; prompt additional review for unusually large plans.                       |
| Planning horizon      | More than 20 days escalates. The request schema accepts 1-30 days.                                                                                                                                                                            | `policy._order_checks`, `schemas.IntentRequest`; require review for longer execution schedules. |
| Overall policy status | Any`BLOCK` makes status `BLOCK`; otherwise any `ESCALATE` makes it `ESCALATE`; otherwise warnings produce `WARN`; otherwise `PASS`. Execution is allowed only when neither blocked nor escalated; warning-only plans are allowed. | `policy.evaluate`; aggregate individual check outcomes.                                           |

## Cash and Allocation

| Rule / threshold           | How it is used                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | Where and why                                                                                                                                                                       |
| -------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Cash reserve               | 1% of AUM is reserved and excluded from investable cash.                                                                                                                                                                                                       | `data.py` / `data_v2.py` builders; fixed demo operating buffer.                                                                                                                 |
| Horizon cash forecast      | Cash - reserve + pending trade cash impacts + dividends payable within horizon - daily expense accrual × horizon + estimated subscriptions - estimated redemptions; floor at zero. Subscription estimate is 0.08% AUM and redemption estimate 0.06% AUM.      | `planner.cash_flow_planning`; estimate plan funding capacity.                                                                                                                     |
| Plan contribution          | Positive contribution is added to forecast investable cash and separately identified. Policy allows that new subscription amount to be fully deployed, while limiting other operational cash usage to 95%. Net buys above allowed cash are`BLOCK`.           | `planner.generate_plan`; `policy._order_checks`; preserve operating cash without stranding new inflow.                                                                          |
| Rule-path minimum ticket   | Contribution and redemption orders under ₹0.50 Cr are skipped; rebalance deltas below ₹0.25 Cr are skipped. These are not universal policy limits: optimizer-generated contributions/redemptions and manual orders do not apply the same per-order minimums. | `planner.build_contribution`, `build_redemption`, `build_rebalance`; reduce small tickets on those paths. `min_order_cr` settings in `policy.POLICY_DEFAULTS` are unused. |
| Whole-share sizing         | Rupee allocation is converted to shares by flooring at the selected price; value is shares × price. Unused cash / shortfall may remain.                                                                                                                       | `planner._order` and builders; avoid fractional shares.                                                                                                                           |
| Rule-based contribution    | Buy eligible underweight holdings toward target in proportion to shortfall; then allocate remainder pro rata to target weights.                                                                                                                                | `planner.build_contribution`; restore target allocation first.                                                                                                                    |
| Rule-based redemption      | Trim overweight holdings in proportion to excess; distribute remaining sale pro rata to current weight; cap at sellable shares. Actual raised amount may be below request.                                                                                     | `planner.build_redemption`; raise payout while correcting overweights.                                                                                                            |
| Rule-based sector increase | Split amount evenly across selected sectors. Within a sector, use configured buy fractions; if absent, allocate among held names by current weight. If buys exceed investable cash, try selling lowest-weight holdings outside target sectors.                 | `planner._orders_by_rules`, `_build_buy_orders`, `_funding_sells`; implement sector intent and find funding.                                                                  |
| Rule-based sector decrease | Split amount evenly across target sectors, then sell highest-weight holdings in each sector first. General raise-cash sells start from lowest-weight positions.                                                                                                | `planner._orders_by_rules`; follow targeted reduction or raise cash from low-weight positions.                                                                                    |
| Rule-based rebalance       | Rescale target weights to the currently invested proportion, trade deltas >=₹0.25 Cr, and respect available sellable shares.                                                                                                                                  | `planner.build_rebalance`; correct relative drift without targeting a fixed cash level.                                                                                           |

Rules-path fixed buy allocation fractions are: IT: INFY 35%, TCS 30%, HCLTECH 15%, LTIM 12%, TECHM 8%; Financial Services: HDFCBANK 40%, ICICIBANK 35%, AXISBANK 25%; Energy: RELIANCE 70%, NTPC 30%; FMCG: ITC 100%; Automobile: MARUTI 100%; Healthcare: SUNPHARMA 60%, CIPLA 40%. Excluded names are removed and remaining fractions renormalized. These allocations are in synthetic `data.BUY_ALLOCATION`, so they are used even when a v2 fund is selected.

## Optimizer Constraints

| Rule / threshold            | How it is used                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | Where and why                                                                                                                                            |
| --------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Buy objective and caps      | Maximize forecast return; long-only. Uses active fund issuer/sector caps; contribution cap basis is pre-trade AUM plus contribution. Initially each name is capped at 34% of ticket. On infeasibility, remove the name cap, then sector cap; issuer cap remains. | `optimizer.optimize_buy`, `planner._orders_by_optimizer`; allocate cash under concentration limits. Relaxed sector cap is left for policy checks.    |
| Sell objective and capacity | Minimize forecast return given up; sell only sellable shares; requested raise capped at total sellable value. Initially each name can fund at most 34% of raise; cap is removed if infeasible.                                                                   | `optimizer.optimize_sell`; raise cash while minimizing forecast return given up.                                                                       |
| Rebalance                   | Long-only and cash-neutral relative to invested book; L1 turnover <=15% of AUM; optimizer issuer cap is 10%.                                                                                                                                                     | `optimizer.optimize_rebalance`; limit churn while tilting toward forecast returns. Generic 10% cap does not honor SBI ETF's configured 13% issuer cap. |
| Solver fallback             | Tries CLARABEL, ECOS, SCS. If CVXPY is unavailable or optimization fails, planner falls back to rules and evaluates the result with policy.                                                                                                                      | `optimizer._solve_continuous`, `planner.generate_plan`; preserve plan generation, but fallback is not equivalent to optimization.                    |
| Forecast inputs             | Hard-coded illustrative 1-month returns; unspecified ticker defaults to +1%.                                                                                                                                                                                     | `forecast.py`; currently a placeholder, not a predictive signal suitable for investment decisions.                                                     |

## Data Assumptions That Affect Rules

| Assumption                   | Effect on decisions                                                                                                                                                                                                    | Source / reason                                                                                                                                                                        |
| ---------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Active cash estimates        | Cash is assumed to be 6%, 3.5%, 1%, 7.5%, and 5% of equity for HDFC Flexi, ICICI Large Cap, SBI ETF, HDFC Retirement, and Kotak Large & Mid Cap. It is not a live cash balance.                                        | `data_v2.CASH_PCT_BY_FUND`; substitute where disclosure cash is absent.                                                                                                              |
| Target-weight normalization  | A positive disclosed percentage below 1 is multiplied by 100. Invalid weights or total target weight outside 50%-105% are replaced with market-value weights based on equity.                                          | `data_v2._build_ds_v2`; recover from disclosure layout variations. Can inflate valid sub-1% weights; equity-based targets and AUM-based current weights have different denominators. |
| Share derivation             | When price and market value exist, derived share formula appears 1,000× smaller than`market_value_lakhs * 100,000 / price`. This can distort sellable/locked shares and dividends.                                  | `data_v2._build_ds_v2`; likely data defect, not a business rule.                                                                                                                     |
| Price/sector source mismatch | Planner checks synthetic`data.UNIVERSE` before active fund holdings, so matching tickers may use synthetic prices/sectors/names in generated orders. This affects share counts, order values and sector attribution. | `planner._price_for`, `_name_for`, `_sector_for`; high-impact mismatch for active v2 plans.                                                                                      |
| Liquidity metadata           | Missing ADV is estimated as max(10 Cr, 8% of position value in Cr); median ADV is 85% of ADV. Spread bands are 0.15% for ADV >=500 Cr, 0.30% for >=100 Cr, 0.60% for >=25 Cr, else 0.95%.                              | `security_metadata.curate_security_metadata`; estimates allow demo checks but may create false confidence.                                                                           |
| Business-day dates           | Date offsets skip weekends only, not exchange holidays. Events, lock expiry and settlement dates are curated/hard-coded.                                                                                               | `data.py` / `data_v2.py:add_business_days`; demo timing, not production calendar data.                                                                                             |

## Material Control Gaps

- The planner's separate concentration summary is not the same as policy evaluation: it uses different status logic, checks only positive issuer deltas, sectors passed by the request, and non-passing group rows. Do not use it alone for approval.
- Final `execution_allowed` is false if policy blocks/escalates or planner concentration checks fail. However, the decision endpoint still permits recording `Approve` regardless of that flag. There is no actual trading integration; approval status is not an execution safeguard.
- Optimizer includes existing holdings in buy candidates before checking exclusions; an excluded holding can be proposed, although policy subsequently blocks it.
- `min_adv_cr`, `min_order_cr`, `min_rebalance_order_cr`, and `soft_warning_ratio` are configured but not enforced. Maximum large-cap and cash limits are also informational only.
- Metadata for tobacco/coal percentages, controversial weapons, `ucits_eligible`, lot size, FX hedging, and corporate-action restrictions is not independently enforced. ESG exclusions are ticker/profile flags, not general exposure-threshold rules.
- Active group exposure is ticker-level due to missing group mapping; policy limits cannot aggregate real business groups until group metadata is supplied.
